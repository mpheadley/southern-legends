Lyle Holland has been an ordained Methodist elder for thirty years in Alabama. Born in Birmingham, raised in Decatur, trained at the University of Alabama in Huntsville and Asbury Theological Seminary in Kentucky.

He has been an associate pastor. A founding pastor. A senior pastor. He has been the interim when congregations needed someone steady while they figured out what came next. He gave messages at Jacksonville State. He has been at Fayette First Methodist in recent years.

Thirty years in a tradition that asks you to move when the bishop says move, and to trust that the room you're walking into will receive you, is a particular kind of formation. Lyle Holland has been formed by it.
