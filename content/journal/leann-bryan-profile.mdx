In September 2020, a car crossed the center line on Leon Smith Parkway in Oxford and hit the minivan carrying Kyle and Leann Bryan and their two children. Lucy was three. She was airlifted to Children's Hospital in Birmingham and did not survive.

Leann Bryan has been living inside that fact ever since. So has Kyle. So has their son Ashley, who was five.

---

The memorial playground at The Children's Place daycare at Anniston First United Methodist Church is one thing they gave away when they had nothing. Lucy loved playgrounds. She would point out every one she saw from the car window. Now there is one in her name, and children who never knew her play on it.

That is what grief looks like when it is made by people who have decided not to let it close them.
