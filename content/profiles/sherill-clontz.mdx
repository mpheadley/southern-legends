She did not start in the church.

She started in management analysis for the Army Material Command. Then she moved into logistics and operations engineering at McDonnell Douglas Space Systems. She was a stay-at-home parent. She got her MBA in Logistics from Alabama A&M. She was baptized at Grace Methodist Church in Gaithersburg, Maryland, which is as far from the Alabama hills as you can get while still being in the same country.

Then somewhere in her forties, she went to Vanderbilt Divinity School. She got her M.Div. and she was licensed as a pastor in 1999.

She has been inside the United Methodist Church's organizational structure ever since, and she has risen through it the way people who came from logistics rise through things — by understanding systems.

---

## What She Runs Now

Sherill Clontz is the District Superintendent of the North District of the North Alabama Annual Conference. She is also Dean of the Cabinet — the senior DS, the one who runs the cabinet-level structure that the bishop operates through.

She has been an Associate Superintendent. She was the DS of the Cheaha District from 2012 to 2016. She has been Conference Secretary. She has been Annual Conference Parliamentarian. She knows the structure of United Methodism in North Alabama the way someone knows a building they have been maintaining for twenty years.

She is also currently pursuing a Doctor of Ministry in Gospel and Culture from Columbia Theological Seminary. She did not stop studying when she got the job.

---

## The Depression She Named

She has written publicly about a period of depression she experienced in her thirties — before the ministry career, in the years of logistics and aerospace and young children. She credits spiritual friendship with pulling her through it.

She called the blog she wrote during that period "Comfortable Being Uncomfortable." The title tells you the core posture. The woman who came from the Army and aerospace and emerged on the other side as a district superintendent running the North District is not someone who expects the road to be smooth.
