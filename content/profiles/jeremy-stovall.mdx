He got his bachelor's at Jacksonville State. Then his master's. Then he went to the University of Alabama for a doctorate in wind conducting. Then he came back to Jacksonville.

That is a pattern. Dr. Jeremy Stovall has spent most of his career inside the same radius. He knows the hills, the campus, the sound the Marching Southerners make when 450 people hit the field together. He has conducted them as a student, taught alongside them as a faculty member, and since January 1, 2026, he leads the whole program.

He is the Director of Bands at Jacksonville State University. He is also a two-time JSU graduate.

---

## The Sweeney Todd Connection

Before he was Director of Bands, Stovall served as Music Director for the JSU Drama Department's musical productions and the Jacksonville Opera Theatre.

In February and March 2009, the JSU drama department mounted *Sweeney Todd: The Demon Barber of Fleet Street* at the Ernest Stone Performing Arts Center. [Jason Wright](/profiles/jason-wright-piedmont-theater) played the title role. Jeremy Stovall conducted the pit orchestra.

That production is well documented in JSU archives. The costume designs are cataloged in JSU Digital Commons. The staging was built from scratch in a building not designed for musical theatre. The musicians were underfoot and the cast was on top of them and somehow it worked.

Stovall had his baton in the pit. Wright was above him on the stage singing Sondheim. That is a collaboration worth naming.

---

## The Thirty-Two Year Inheritance

In September 2025, Dr. Ken Bodiford — who had run the JSU bands program for thirty-two years — announced his retirement effective January 1, 2026.

Stovall was named his successor.

The Marching Southerners had grown from roughly 144 members to 450+ under Bodiford. The program Stovall inherited is the largest it has ever been.

The 2020 JSU National Alumni Association named Stovall Outstanding Faculty Member of the Year. He teaches Conducting and Marching Band Techniques. He runs the Symphonic Band and the Pep Band. He is the new steward of something Bodiford spent three decades building.

---

*Dr. Jeremy Stovall is the Director of Bands at Jacksonville State University and an Associate Professor in the David L. Walters Department of Music. He is a two-time JSU graduate. His mentor was [Dr. Ken Bodiford](/profiles/ken-bodiford).*
