Scott Whitney describes himself as a sports guy who came to theater through his kids.

That is the whole story, and also the beginning of it. He is a physical therapist assistant working in the Anniston and Oxford area. He has no formal theater training. He came to CAST — Community Actors' Studio Theatre, Anniston's community theater company — because his daughter Ramsey was doing it, and then he stayed.

He played Gomez in *The Addams Family*. He played Doolittle in *My Fair Lady*. He played Edward Bloom in *Big Fish*. He played Daddy Warbucks in *Annie* — alongside Ramsey, who played Annie. He has said that sharing the stage with her was the greatest moment of his time in theater.

He also runs sound. He took on the Program Director role for CAST Kidz, the children's troupe, and for CAST Academy of Performing Arts. He directed *The Jungle Book Kids* at the Anniston Performing Arts Center. His own description of why he does it: "I am thankful for a program such as CAST that allows me to be creative, inspire children, entertain people, and share joy from the stage."

The sports guy who showed up for his kid found a whole second life.

## Emmalie

Emmalie Whitney is the Executive Director of Family and Children Ministry at Anniston First United Methodist Church. She runs the week — the children's programming, the staff, the devotionals she delivers herself for three- and four-year-olds at Wednesday chapel. She calls herself a "momager," a term that captures the skill set accurately: the person who organizes everyone's schedule, manages operations, and makes the thing actually happen.

On weekends, she directs theater.

Her directing credits at CAST include *All is Calm* (2022 and 2023), *Murder on the Orient Express*, and *Blithe Spirit* — Noel Coward's comedy of manners about a ghost and a very inconvenient séance, staged in September 2025 at Garfrerick's Café in Oxford. Four mainstage directing projects. The skill set transfers: the same person who manages a church children's department is the same person who manages a cast of actors and a production crew, because both require the ability to hold the plan and the chaos in the same hand without dropping either.

## Ramsey

Their daughter Ramsey started at CAST young and did not stop.

She performed with CAST and with CharACTers, Gadsden's community theater troupe. At eleven years old, she landed a role in a national Bojangles television commercial. After the commercial aired, agents in Los Angeles expressed interest in representing her for television and film.

She is from Anniston.

---

## The Church, the Stage, the Kids

The Whitneys are connected to Anniston First United Methodist Church — Emmalie on staff, Scott through the community. They are also embedded in CAST, which is the town's other community institution with a green room. The overlap is not incidental. Both the church and the theater are asking the same question of the people who show up: who do you want to be when you're not performing the regular version of yourself?

The Whitneys have apparently found compatible answers in both rooms.
