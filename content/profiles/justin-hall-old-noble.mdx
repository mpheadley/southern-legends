Justin Hall owns Old Noble. It has no website.

That is not a failure of marketing. That is a specific posture toward the internet — the one that says: the food is the thing, the room is the thing, and the people who need to know about it already know. Everyone else will find out because someone they trust told them.

Old Noble is on Noble Street in Anniston. Noble Street is the long avenue that has been many things — commerce, decline, conversation, slow revival — and is now in the middle of deciding what it wants to be next. Old Noble is one of the restaurants that has become part of what that street is becoming.

---

## The Following

Justin Hall does not have a website. He has a very active Facebook page.

That distinction matters. A website is for search engines and strangers. A Facebook page is for people who have already decided they care about the place — who want to know what the special is today, who want to see the photos before they go, who want the feeling of being a regular even when they haven't been in a few weeks.

The people who follow Old Noble on Facebook are the people who drive from Oxford and Gadsden to eat there. That is not an accident. That is what a loyal local following looks like — people who go out of their way because the experience is worth it.

---

## Noble Street

The street is named for something. It has been trying to live up to the name for a long time.

Justin Hall's restaurant is part of what it has become. A place to go on a Friday night, a place worth the drive, a place with a regular table for people who know what they want.

Old Noble. No website. Very active Facebook. Worth finding.
