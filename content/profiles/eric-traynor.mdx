He teaches in the Department of Theatre and Film at Jacksonville State University.

His students call him approachable, fun, someone who "really knows drama." On Rate My Professors — which is, for all its limitations, a place where students say true things — he carries a 4.6 rating and a difficulty score that suggests he makes the hard things feel accessible.

He directed *A Tennessee Walk* at the Stone Center in June 2013 — the production won the Southern Playwright Contest. He directed *Seussical, the Musical* in 2007. He has been in the world of JSU theatrical production long enough to have seen the program through multiple eras.

## The Sweeney Todd Connection

In the [Jason Got Sweeney](/essays/jason-got-sweeney) essay on this site, Matt Headley writes about the 2009 JSU production of *Sweeney Todd: The Demon Barber of Fleet Street* at the Ernest Stone Performing Arts Center.

Eric Traynor was in the room for that production — a member of the JSU theater community who has been present for more of what happens on that stage than most people in this region ever get to witness.

The 2009 Sweeney Todd is one of those productions that people who were there have not forgotten. [Jason Wright](/profiles/jason-wright-piedmont-theater) played the title role. [Jeremy Stovall](/profiles/jeremy-stovall) was in the pit with the orchestra. It was a full production of Sondheim in a regional university theater, mounted from scratch by people who decided it was worth doing.

Eric Traynor knew what it was worth. He was there.
