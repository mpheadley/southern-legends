She hosted *Alabama Traveler* on local television. She co-hosted *Wake Up Alabama*. She had a face that people in Calhoun County and Etowah County recognized from the screen.

Then she retired from Jacksonville State University, where she had worked for decades — Assistant Director of Development, Alumni Director, Assistant Director of JSU-Gadsden. She had been on both sides of the screen and the desk and the podium, and at some point she landed in a pew at Jacksonville First United Methodist Church, where she has been a member since January 1991.

She sings in the Chancel Choir. She rings in the Handbell Choir. She helps with the children's Christmas program every year. She handles visitor follow-up and new member involvement. She directs funerals. She supports the pastor with administrative work and adult studies.

She is on staff, half-time, at a church in her town — doing the kind of work that keeps the thing alive from the inside out.

---

## The Hug

On a Sunday in July 2026, Matt Headley played guitar at the traditional worship service at Jacksonville First United Methodist Church.

Connie Thompson hugged him after the service.

Some stories are exactly what they are. A community that says welcome, in the way communities say welcome when they have had time to learn how. A woman who has been in that congregation for thirty-five years, who has seen people come and go and come back, who knows the difference between a visitor and someone who is finding their way home.

She knew. She hugged him.

---

*Connie Thompson is a former local television personality (Alabama Traveler, Wake Up Alabama) and retired JSU staff member. She is an active member of Jacksonville First United Methodist Church, where she serves in music ministry and pastoral support roles. She has been a member since January 1991. Her husband is Evin Thompson.*
