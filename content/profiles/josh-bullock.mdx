Josh Bullock is what happens when someone with serious musical training decides to stay.

He is a college roommate of Matt Headley's — which is to say: he is the kind of friend where the origin story starts in a dorm room and the relationship continues across decades of adult life, the way friendships from that time either do or don't. Josh Bullock's does.

He plays organ. He has composed for choirs. He has written. He works in real estate. He has built a life in Northeast Alabama that runs across multiple disciplines, which is what people in small cities sometimes do when they have more capacity than a single profession can contain.

---

## The Organ

An organist in a small Southern city is a specific kind of presence. The organ is the instrument that requires the building — you don't carry it home, you don't play it in a garage, you have to go to the room where the pipe organ is and that room is almost always a church.

Josh Bullock has been in those rooms. He has played the instrument that fills the sanctuary and makes the congregation feel something they can't quite name. That is what organs do, and it takes someone who knows what they're doing.

---

## The Composer

He has also composed — choir music, which means music written for a group of human voices doing something together that none of them could do alone. Choral composition is a specific form of generosity: you write music that other people perform, and the performance is the thing, not you.

Josh Bullock writes that kind of music.

---

## Anniston

He has stayed in Northeast Alabama. That is not nothing. The choice to stay — to build something here rather than leave for a larger city that might have more opportunities for someone with his range — is a kind of commitment to the place. Anniston has him. The churches have him at the organ. The choirs have his compositions. The real estate clients have his attention.

He stayed.
