He came to Jacksonville State in 1994. He started with 144 people in the Marching Southerners.

He left with 450.

Dr. Kenneth G. Bodiford spent thirty-two years growing the bands program at Jacksonville State University. He came in as Director of Bands, built it, and handed it off to his successor [Dr. Jeremy Stovall](/profiles/jeremy-stovall) on January 1, 2026. The program is the size it is today because he had three decades to make it that way.

---

## The Record

He grew up in Alabama. He got his undergraduate degree from JSU itself — the institution he would spend his career building. He got his master's in music education and wind ensemble conducting from East Carolina University. He got his doctorate in instrumental conducting from the University of Alabama.

He came back north.

Under his direction, the JSU Chamber Winds performed and competed nationally. He worked as a clinician and adjudicator and guest conductor on a schedule that took him well beyond Calhoun County. In 2009, the United States House of Representatives entered the Marching Southerners' fall production — titled "Of Thee I Sing" — into the Congressional Record. That is a specific kind of official acknowledgment that not many band programs get.

His successor inherits a program three times the size it was when he started.

## The Institutional Gravity

Thirty-two years at one institution is a particular kind of commitment. It means you outlast the students you started with, the colleagues who hired you, the administrators who set your first budget. The institution changes around you, and you become one of the things it changes around.

He also served as music director for JSU drama department productions and the Jacksonville Opera Theatre — including the 2009 *Sweeney Todd* production conducted by Jeremy Stovall, who learned the craft in part by watching Bodiford's version of institutional leadership for over two decades.

He is retired now. The institution he built is still going.

---

*Dr. Ken Bodiford is the former Director of Bands and Associate Professor of Music at Jacksonville State University, having served from 1994 to January 2026 (32 years). His successor is [Dr. Jeremy Stovall](/profiles/jeremy-stovall). He can be found at kbodiford.com.*
