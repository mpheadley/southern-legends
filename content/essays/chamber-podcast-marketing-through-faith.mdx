I joined Ashlee Jones and the Calhoun County Area Chamber & Visitors Center on their podcast, *In Tune with Calhoun*, for an episode they titled **"Marketing Through Faith."**

We talked about the part of building a business that nobody puts on the highlight reel — how the 24/7 life of a founder wears on your mental health over time, and why I've come to believe the hero's journey is really about redemption, not winning. We got into marketing and faith and the plain work of saying the true thing, but mostly it was an honest conversation about doing this in a small place, among people you know.

I'm grateful the Chamber makes room for that kind of talk.
