This one I know from the inside. I run a small studio, and I've spent real time this year working with local print and sign shops on shirts, banners, and merch. Here's what I've learned: the good ones will tell you the truth about a file before they run it, they'll ask about your deadline before they quote you, and they answer the phone. Big-box online printing is cheaper on paper and worse on everything that matters when it's local. If you're a church, a nonprofit, or a small business here, start with a shop on this list before you upload to a website in another state.
