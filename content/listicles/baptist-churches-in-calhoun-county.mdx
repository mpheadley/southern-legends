I'm a pastor, so I'll be honest about the limits of a list like this. A dataset can tell you a church exists. It can't tell you whether you'll be known there on a Tuesday when things fall apart. Baptist churches anchor more of this county than any other tradition, from the big ones on the main roads to the small missionary Baptist congregations that have held a block together for a hundred years. If you're church-shopping, don't read this as a ranking. Read it as a door list. Go visit three. Stay where you're missed when you're gone.
